# [Ca(amd)2]2 — bis(N,N'-di-i-propylformamidinato)calcium(II) dimer: verified source record

All numbers below were read from the retrieved sources listed at the end. Anything not
retrieved is marked NOT FOUND. Nothing here is inferred except where explicitly labelled
"inference".

## 1. Solid-state structure

Source: Kim et al., Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2016 (SI Tables S1–S3; main text Fig. 1).

- Yes — dimer in the solid state: [(eta2-L)Ca(mu-eta2:eta2-L)(mu-eta2:eta1-L)Ca(eta2-L)],
  L = N,N'-di-i-propylformamidinate. Formula of the dimer Ca2C28H60N8, MW 589.00.
- Ca...Ca = **3.323 Å** (Table S2, "Ca(1)...Ca(2)").
- Coordination number of Ca: **six at Ca(1)** (one terminal kappa2-N,N ligand + two bridging
  ligands: one mu-eta2:eta2 and one mu-eta2:eta1) and **five at Ca(2)**; the sixth site at
  Ca(2) is occupied by an agostic C-H interaction (Ca...H 2.798 and 2.836 Å, Ca...C(24)
  3.102(6) Å; main text calls 2.80/2.84 Å "long end" of agostic range).
- Asymmetry: N(5)/N(6) bridge symmetrically (mu-eta2:eta2); N(7)/N(8) bridge asymmetrically
  (mu-eta2:eta1), Ca(1)-N(8) 2.407(4) Å vs Ca(2)-N(8) 3.545(4) Å (nonbonding).
- Ca-N distances: terminal 2.324(3)-2.374(3) Å; bridging 2.400(3)-2.633(3) Å.
- Bite angles N-Ca-N: 58.04(10)/58.83(10) deg (terminal), 51.88(13)-54.26(13) deg (bridging).
- Crystal data (Table S1): monoclinic, P2(1)/c; a = 10.006(5), b = 18.368(8), c = 20.699(9) Å,
  beta = 99.344(10) deg, V = 3754(3) Å3, Z = 4, T = 100(2) K, Mo Kalpha,
  R1 = 0.0561 (I>2sigma), wR2 = 0.1511, GOF 1.022. Bridging ligands disordered (major
  component SOF 0.846). Crystals grown by sublimation under reduced pressure.
- Solution: 1H/13C NMR shows the dimer is retained in C6D6 (main text).
- Compound 2 (acetamidinate analogue) is also dimeric by connectivity, but its data sets
  could not be refined to a publishable model (disorder/weak diffraction) — no CIF.

## 2. Synthesis paper (Crossref-verified)

Kim, S. B.; Yang, C.; Powers, T.; Davis, L. M.; Lou, X.; Gordon, R. G.
"Synthesis of Calcium(II) Amidinate Precursors for Atomic Layer Deposition through a Redox
Reaction between Calcium and Amidines."
Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2016, 55 (35), 10228-10233. DOI 10.1002/anie.201602406
(Crossref title match confirmed; open access; PMC5094530).
German edition: Angew. Chem. 2016, 128, 10384-10389, DOI 10.1002/ange.201602406 (verified).

Synthesis: Ca metal (dendritic) + 2 equiv N,N'-diisopropylformamidine in THF/liquid NH3,
-78 degC -> rt; 57% yield. Purified by sublimation, 0.1 Torr, 95-100 degC (SI); mp 110-116 degC;
reported to "distill at 110-115 degC, 0.2 torr" (main text).

## 3. Bubbler / sublimation temperatures and vapor pressure

Retrieved numbers (temperature only; no pressure-unit vapor pressure was found):

- Kim 2016 (CaS ALD, H2S): "the precursor was maintained at 140 degC" (main text); the SI ALD
  section states "the source temperature of 1 was kept at 130 degC" — the paper is internally
  inconsistent (130 vs 140 degC). CaS ALD window 150-280 degC; growth 0.87 Å/cycle.
- Hennessy, Rodriguez, West, Brandon, J. Vac. Sci. Technol. A 41(4), 042405 (2023),
  DOI 10.1116/6.0002563 (Crossref-verified): "The Ca precursor material was held in a
  stainless steel bubbler and heated to 140 degC"; substrate 175-250 degC; 0.3-0.5 Å/cycle;
  anhydrous HF co-reactant.
- US 11,111,578 B1 (Elam, Mane, Gebhard; UChicago Argonne; granted 2021-09-07):
  "[Ca(amd)2]2 ... was maintained at 131 degC in a stainless steel bubbler", manifold at
  145 degC, reactor 75-300 degC, standard depositions 225 degC, HF-pyridine co-reactant.
- Amoah, Baumgart, Jin, Wheeler, McDonald, Hunt, ECS Meet. Abstr. MA2022-02, 1126 (2022),
  DOI 10.1149/MA2022-02311126mtgabs (Crossref-verified): describes [Ca(famd)2] as a
  "low vapor pressure solid" that "cannot be reacted by ALD in vapor draw mode"; required a
  150 mL booster bubbler with dip tube and an LVPD kit. No temperature or pressure value in
  the abstract.
- TGA (Kim 2016): quantitative single-step weight loss 150-275 degC (10 degC/min);
  300-400 degC for larger sample/faster ramp; thermally stable to at least 350 degC.
  Evaporation rate ~37x that of [Ca3(tmhd)6] at 225 degC (isothermal TGA, mg min-1 cm-2;
  plotted, not tabulated).

NOT FOUND: any published vapor pressure of [Ca(amd)2]2 in pressure units (Torr/Pa), and any
published enthalpy of sublimation/vaporization. The literature reports evaporation rates
(mg min-1 cm-2) and working bubbler temperatures only.

## 5. Gas-phase structure

NOT FOUND. No experimental gas-phase structure (electron diffraction, microwave, IR/rotational)
and no published computed (DFT/ab initio) geometry for [Ca(amd)2]2 or its monomer were located.

What the literature does say (evidence, not structure):
- NMR (C6D6) shows the dimer is retained in solution (Kim 2016).
- TGA shows single-step, quantitative weight loss in 150-275 degC, i.e. the compound
  vaporizes without decomposition on the TGA timescale (Kim 2016). Whether the vapor is the
  intact dimer or a monomer is not established experimentally in any retrieved source —
  inference only.
